SubShare — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026 · Home

What we store

SubShare is a small social app for sharing the YouTube channels you love with your friends. We store only what is needed to run it:

  • Account: your Google name, email and avatar when you sign in with Google (or email + password if you choose email sign-up).
  • Profiles & content: your username, display name, bio, and the collections / channels you create or share.
  • YouTube subscriptions: only if you connect YouTube (read-only). SubShare can list your subscriptions — it can never post, comment, or change anything on your account.

What we do with your data

  • Your collections are shared only with the people you choose (public / selected friends / private — you decide).
  • Imported channels stay private until you add them to a collection.
  • OAuth tokens for the YouTube connection are encrypted (AES-GCM) before storage and are never readable by other users.

What we never do

  • We do not run ads, analytics or trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not post on your YouTube or Google account.

Your control

You can disconnect YouTube, delete your collections, or delete your account at any time. Data you delete is removed from our database.
